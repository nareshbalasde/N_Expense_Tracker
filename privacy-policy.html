Privacy Policy — N Expense Tracker (NET)

Last updated: August 2026

N Expense Tracker ("NET", "the app") is a personal expense-tracking app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data collection

NET does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission for user data and does not use analytics, ads, or third-party trackers.

Data storage

All information you enter — expenses, income, budgets, categories, and notes — is stored only on your device, using local on-device storage. This data never leaves your phone and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

File import

The app lets you import a text file from your device to add expense entries. This file is read locally on your device to create entries; it is not uploaded anywhere.

PDF export / printing

The app can generate a report and hand it to Android's built-in print system so you can save it as a PDF or print it. This uses Android's native print service and does not send your data over the internet.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries within the app, or clear all app data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → N Expense Tracker → Storage → Clear Data. Uninstalling the app also removes all its data from your device.

Children's privacy

NET does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the Play Store listing page.
