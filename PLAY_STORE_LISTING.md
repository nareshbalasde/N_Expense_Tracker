## Full description (max 4000 characters)
NET (N Expense Tracker) is a simple, private expense tracker that keeps all your data on your own device — no accounts, no internet required, no ads.

FEATURES
• Log expenses and income by category (Food, Transport, Bills, Rent, Shopping, Health, and more)
• Set an overall monthly budget and per-category budgets, with a visual progress meter
• See daily totals and a monthly category breakdown at a glance
• Import a whole month of expenses at once from a simple text file
• Export a clean report as a PDF anytime, straight from the app
• Light and dark themes
• 100% offline — your financial data never leaves your phone

PRIVACY FIRST
NET doesn't ask for accounts, doesn't use ads or trackers, and doesn't send your data anywhere. Everything is stored locally on your device.

Built for anyone who wants a fast, no-fuss way to see where their money is going each month.
